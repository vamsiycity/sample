**Effective Date:** 14 February 2026

We may collect the following types of information:

### a) Personal Information
When users register or log in to the application, we may collect:
- Name
- Student/Teacher ID or Roll Number
- Email address
- Mobile number
- Class/Section details
- Parent/Guardian information (if applicable)

### b) Device & Technical Information
We may automatically collect certain information including:
- Device model
- Operating system version
- App version
- IP address
- Device identifiers
- Log data (crash reports, performance diagnostics)

### c) Usage Information
We may collect usage activity such as:
- Login time
- Features accessed
- Timetable views
- Announcement views
- Notification interactions

## 2. How We Use the Information

We use the collected information to:

- Provide and maintain the App
- Allow users to access timetable, announcements, and calendar
- Send school notifications and important alerts
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Respond to support requests
- Ensure security and prevent unauthorized access

We **do not sell** user personal data to third parties.

---

## 3. Push Notifications

The app uses push notifications to send:
- School announcements
- Timetable updates
- Holiday notices
- Important alerts

Users can disable notifications anytime from device settings.

We do **not** share personal information with third parties except:
